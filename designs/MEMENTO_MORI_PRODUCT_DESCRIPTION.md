### The Story Behind the Design

This design emerged from the intersection of two powerful aesthetics: the introspective darkness of gothic memento mori tradition and the life-affirming beauty of cottage core's botanical obsession.

In medieval and Victorian times, memento mori art served as reminder of mortality's inevitability. But what if, instead of fear, we met that reality with appreciation? What if death wasn't an ending but a transformation—a skull becoming a planter, bones becoming soil, endings becoming beginnings?

The wildflowers aren't random:
- **Lavender** for devotion and serenity
- **Roses** for love transcending mortality
- **Forget-me-nots** for remembrance and eternal connection
- **Daisies** for innocence and new beginnings

The **moon phases** remind us that all things cycle—from new to full and back again, from life to death to life.

This is memento mori reimagined: not "remember you will die," but "remember to truly live."
